How to Play

Guess My Number — Dev 'Lin Edition'

🎯
GoalBe the first to guess your opponent's secret number.
🔢
SetupYou and Dev Lin each pick a hidden number in the chosen range — Easy 0–50, Normal 0–100, or Hardcore 0–1000.
📱
Your boardThe left board marked YOU is yours — type your guesses there. The right board marked BOT just shows what Dev Lin is doing.
🔁
Taking turnsYou guess first, then Dev Lin. After each guess you're told Higher (the secret is bigger), Lower (smaller), or Correct.
⚡
Final Guess Phase (automatic)Once the window narrows to within 8 (Easy), 10 (Normal) or 12 (Hardcore), hints stop. Replies become only Correct or Wrong, and you must guess inside that window, one number at a time.
🏆
WinningThe first hunter to land Correct wins. You always move first each round, so a sharp search gives you the edge!
🌙
Day / NightTap the moon / sun button any time to switch between the sky theme and the galaxy night theme.
